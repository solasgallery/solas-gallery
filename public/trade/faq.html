Frequently Asked Questions

Trade Collective Partnership Guide

Who qualifies for the Trade Collective?
+
Interior designers, decorators and other design professionals. Tell us about your practice on the application form and we will take it from there.
What are the membership tiers?
+
There are three tiers: Trade, Trade Plus and Trade Premier, each with a higher trade discount. Your tier is set by your purchases over a rolling 12 months: Trade at $2,500+ a year (10%), Trade Plus at $7,500+ (15%) and Trade Premier at $15,000+ (20%).
Is there a cost to join?
+
There is no membership fee. The Trade Collective is a partnership, not a subscription.
What does trade pricing cover?
+
Trade pricing runs from 10% to 20%, by tier.
How does the application process work?
+
Fill in the application form on this site. We reply within five business days.
Can I bring my clients to the gallery?
+
Yes. The gallery is open Monday to Saturday, 10 to 5. Ask us — we'll tell you what we can do for your project.
Do you handle installation?
+
Our pieces are framed, delivered and hung on your wall. Ask us — we'll tell you what we can do for your project.
What about custom commissions?
+
Commissions are made from your images, including heirloom folio and albums, in our studio in Salado. Ask us — we'll tell you what we can do for your project.
Do you serve commercial and hospitality projects?
+
Ask us — we'll tell you what we can do for your project.
Where is the gallery?
+
2 Rock Creek Dr, Salado, TX 76571, on Main Street at the corner of Main and Rock Creek. Call (254) 947-1881.
