Frequently Asked Questions

Trade Collective Partnership Guide

Who qualifies for the Trade Collective?
+
Licensed interior designers, registered architects, and accredited design professionals with an active practice. We review each application for alignment with our curatorial standards. Membership is not exclusive to ASID members, though professional credentials strengthen your application.
What are the membership tiers?
+
The program offers three tiers — Associate, Preferred, and Atelier — each with increasing trade considerations, services, and co-marketing benefits. All new members begin at Associate and advance based on project volume and annual engagement.
Is there a cost to join?
+
There is no membership fee. The Trade Collective is a partnership, not a subscription. Your investment is in the art you source for your clients — we invest in supporting your practice.
What trade considerations are available?
+
Trade considerations range from 15% to 25% on gallery inventory, with additional considerations on custom commissions, framing through Salado Village Framer, and large-format giclée printing. Specific rates correspond to your partnership tier.
How does the application process work?
+
Submit your application online or via our printable form. Our team reviews applications within 48–72 hours. Once approved, your dedicated concierge schedules an introductory gallery tour to explore how we can best serve your practice.
Can I bring my clients to the gallery?
+
Absolutely — private gallery tours are a core benefit. We curate selections based on your project brief, prepare scaled renderings, and create a memorable experience for your clients. After-hours and weekend availability increases with tier level.
Do you handle installation?
+
Yes. We offer white-glove delivery and professional installation. For Preferred and Atelier members, installation is complimentary on qualifying orders.
What about custom commissions?
+
We facilitate bespoke commissions through our artist network. This includes artist introductions, concept development, progress updates, and final delivery. Custom work is available at preferential trade rates.
Do you serve commercial and hospitality projects?
+
Yes. Our large-format giclée printing capabilities (up to 44 inches wide), edition production, and volume considerations make us well-suited for commercial, hospitality, and multi-unit residential projects.
What geographic area do you serve?
+
We serve designers throughout Central Texas — including Austin, Round Rock, Georgetown, Temple, Killeen, Waco, and the surrounding Hill Country — with shipping available nationwide for larger projects.
