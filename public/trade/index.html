Designer Partnership Excellence

Designed for Your Practice

The Solas Gallery Trade Collective offers curated partnership opportunities for interior designers, decorators, and design professionals who serve discerning clients. We understand the unique needs of your practice and provide dedicated support, professional pricing structures, and exclusive access to our collections.

Our three-tier program accommodates practices of all sizes, from emerging designers to established studios. Each tier unlocks distinct benefits tailored to your business needs and project volume.

Why Partner with Solas

  • Curated collections for discerning clients
  • Professional trade consideration structures
  • Dedicated design consultation support
  • Priority access to new acquisitions
  • Flexible project-based arrangements
  • Support for residential and commercial projects

Trade Services & Support

Design Consultation

Professional guidance on art selection, color palettes, and spatial design integration. Our curators work directly with your design team to ensure perfect alignment with project vision and client aesthetic.

Custom Sourcing

We source specific pieces for your projects, drawing from our collections and trusted network of artists. Whether you need a particular style, scale, or subject matter, we handle the research and curation.

Project Pricing

Transparent, competitive trade consideration based on project scope and partnership tier. Simplified invoicing and flexible payment terms designed to support your project management workflow.

Priority Access

First access to new acquisitions and exclusive pieces. Gallery events and collection previews give your practice competitive advantage in serving high-end clientele.

Installation Support

Professional handling, logistics coordination, and installation guidance. We work seamlessly with your teams to ensure flawless project delivery and client satisfaction.

Account Management

Dedicated account support for ongoing relationships. Your primary contact understands your design aesthetic, client base, and project needs for streamlined collaboration.

Partnership Tiers

Choose the tier that best aligns with your practice size and project volume. All tiers include dedicated support and professional pricing.

Associate

15%

Entry-level partnership ideal for emerging practices and occasional project needs.

  • Trade consideration on all pieces
  • Quarterly collection updates
  • Email consultation support
  • Invoice payment terms
  • Event invitations

Preferred

20%

Our most popular tier for active design practices with regular project flow.

  • All Associate benefits
  • Priority new acquisition access
  • Phone & in-person consultation
  • Monthly collection features
  • Custom sourcing service
  • Dedicated account manager

Atelier

25%-35%

Premium partnership for high-volume studios and design firms.

  • All Preferred benefits
  • Exclusive collection access
  • Direct curator partnership
  • Commissioned piece sourcing
  • Private viewing events
  • Flexible project agreements
  • VIP client experiences
